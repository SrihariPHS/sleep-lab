Best White Noise Machines in Australia (2026)

3 machines evaluated · AU voltage & plug notes · updated August 2026

White noise is one of the few sleep aids with genuine research behind it, but buying a machine in Australia adds two questions the US reviews never answer: will it work on 240 V mains with an AU plug, and can you actually buy it here without paying import prices? These three machines clear both bars.

What's different about buying in Australia

Australian mains runs at 230–240 V with the AS/NZS 3112 plug — the flat angled pins. Most machines sold locally handle this fine, but two traps catch parents:

US imports. Amazon AU listings often drop-ship the US version. A machine that ships with a US plug will need an adapter, and one that doesn't accept 100–240 V will need a step-down converter (an adapter alone is not enough). Check the listing's plug note before ordering.

USB-C devices sidestep the problem. Anything that charges over USB-C runs on any Australian phone charger, which makes the portable category much easier to buy locally than the plug-in category.

Red Nose Australia safe-sleep rules still apply

Red Nose Australia's safe-sleep guidance doesn't single out white noise machines, but its principles cover them: the machine belongs out of the cot and out of reach, ideally at least 200 cm from where baby sleeps, and never at maximum volume. If you can hear it clearly from outside the closed nursery door, it's too loud. None of this changes the fundamentals — back to sleep, on a firm flat surface, with no loose bedding.

AU note: keep the room around 18–20°C per Red Nose guidance, and remember a sound machine is an aid, not a fix for an unsafe sleep environment. If the machine needs an adapter, tape the adapter to the cord so it doesn't end up lost (or worse, near the cot).

Frequently asked questions

What voltage are white noise machines in Australia?

Australia runs 230–240V with AS/NZS plugs. The three machines we recommend (Dohm Classic, Hatch Rest Go, LectroFan EVO) all have AU-compatible power options — check the listing says 240V or 'AU plug' before buying.

Where can I buy white noise machines in Australia?

All three picks are available through Amazon Australia and major baby retailers (Baby Bunting, Baby Village). AU pricing is roughly A$60–120 for the budget pick, A$100–180 for the others.

What does Red Nose Australia say about white noise?

Red Nose's safe-sleep guidance focuses on back-to-sleep, a firm flat surface and no loose bedding. White noise is not part of their core guidance — it's a tool parents can use within safe volume limits, not a safety device.
