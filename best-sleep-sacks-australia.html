What to look for in Australia

Red Nose Australia's safe-sleep guidance is the baseline: no loose bedding in the cot, baby on the back, head uncovered, room around 18–20°C. A sleep sack is the only blanket-like layer that satisfies all of that. The fit rule matters most: the sack must be snug at the chest with no room for baby to slip down inside — if baby can slide, size down.

One Australian quirk: local brands size sacks by weight more than age (2.5–6 kg, 6–12 kg), so check the kg range on the packet, not the cute number of months on the front. If you're new to TOG ratings, our TOG guide has the full room-temperature chart.

Frequently asked questions

Which sleep sacks are Australian?

Bonds (Wondersuit), Love To Dream and Ergobaby all have strong Australian availability — Love To Dream is Australian-founded. Woolino and Tommee Tippee are imported but widely stocked at Baby Bunting and online.

What does Red Nose recommend for sleep sacks?

Red Nose Australia recommends a safe infant sleeping bag (well-fitting, with the neck and armholes snug, and no hood) as an alternative to blankets. The room-temperature guidance is 18–20°C.

What size sleep sack does my baby need?

Size by weight, not age — sacks are sized for weight ranges (e.g. 2.5–6kg, 6–12kg). The sack must be snug at the chest with no room for baby to slip down inside.
