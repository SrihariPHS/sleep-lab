Best Sleep Sacks & Wearable Blankets (2026)

10 sacks evaluated · TOG explained · updated August 2026

A sleep sack is the only blanket that's safe for a baby under 12 months — loose bedding is a known SIDS risk factor. The real questions are TOG (warmth), fit, and whether the sack survives the laundry cycle. Here's the shortlist.

Why sleep sacks, and what TOG means

Safe-sleep guidance is consistent across Australia, the UK and the US: no loose blankets in the cot for babies under 12 months. A wearable blanket removes that risk while keeping baby warm. TOG is the warmth rating — roughly: 0.5 TOG for hot summer nights, 1.0 for standard room temperature, 2.5 for winter. The rule that matters more than any rating: check the room temperature and dress underneath accordingly — overheating is itself a SIDS risk factor.
