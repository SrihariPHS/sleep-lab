Best White Noise Machines in the UK (2026)

3 machines evaluated · UK voltage & plug notes · updated August 2026

White noise is one of the few sleep aids with real research behind it, but most buying advice is written for the US market. This guide answers the UK-specific questions: will it run on 240 V mains with a three-pin plug, where can you actually buy it, and what does it cost in pounds?

What's different about buying in the UK

UK mains runs at 230–240 V with the BS 1363 three-pin plug. Most machines sold here handle that natively, but two traps catch parents:

US imports. Amazon UK listings sometimes ship the US version. A machine with a US two-pin plug needs an adapter, and one that doesn't accept 100–240 V needs a step-down converter — an adapter alone won't save it. Always check the listing's plug and voltage note.

USB-C sidesteps the problem. Anything that charges over USB-C runs on any UK phone charger, which makes the portable category much easier to buy locally.

Lullaby Trust safer-sleep rules still apply

The Lullaby Trust's safer-sleep guidance — a clear cot, baby on the back, room around 16–20°C, no loose bedding — doesn't mention sound machines, but its principles cover them: the machine belongs out of the cot and out of reach, ideally at least 200 cm from where baby sleeps, and never at maximum volume. If you can hear it clearly from outside the closed nursery door, it's too loud.

UK note: if the machine needs a plug adapter, tape the adapter to the cord so it stays put and stays away from the cot. And remember a sound machine is an aid, not a fix for an unsafe sleep environment.
